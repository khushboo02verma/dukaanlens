About

A test company, on purpose.

DukaanLens is not a real business. It is a demo venture built to test Growth OS, a growth platform, on a company other than a live one. Every tool Growth OS connects to (Google Analytics, Search Console, Tag Manager, a leads sheet, Microsoft Clarity and a Google Ads test account) is set up on this site, so the whole flow can be tried without touching a real company's website.

What is real here

The problem is real: FMCG brands in India struggle to know what is on the shelf of each kirana store. The product described here is an idea. There are no customers, no results and no prices, and nothing on this site claims otherwise.

Why a shelf-audit idea

People search for shelf audit and retail execution software, and real companies already sell it. That gives Growth OS real search results, rivals and buyer questions to read, which is what a test needs.
