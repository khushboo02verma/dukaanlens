Shelf and cooler audit for beverage brands

Shelves and coolers: how beverage brands can check what a kirana store's cooler and shelf actually hold.

Coolers are part of the shelf

A branded cooler filled with rival drinks is a common gap; the cooler is photographed like a shelf.

Summer peaks

Out-of-stock in peak months costs the most; checks can run more often then.

Many pack sizes

Each size of each drink is counted, so the right pack can be reordered.

What gets checked

  • Out-of-stock detection: Find which of your products are missing from each kirana store's shelf the same day, from one photo your salesman already takes.
  • Share of shelf: Measure how much of each shelf your brand holds next to rivals, in every kirana store your team visits, from shelf photos.
  • Planogram compliance: Check whether your products sit where the agreed shelf layout says, without a merchandiser filling a form after the visit.
  • Price label check: Find stores where the shelf price label does not match your price or a running scheme, from the same shelf photo.
