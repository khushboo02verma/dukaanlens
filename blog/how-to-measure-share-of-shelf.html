How to measure share of shelf in kirana stores

DukaanLens team · 4 min read

Share of shelf is the part of a category's shelf space your brand holds. It is what the shopper sees, which is why it often moves before sales and market share do.

Two ways to count it

  • By facings: count the product fronts facing the shopper. Simple and works on photos.
  • By shelf length: measure the width your products take. More exact, harder in crowded stores.

Doing it in kirana stores

Kirana shelves are crowded and change often, so a count from memory is rarely right. Counting facings from a photo taken in the visit is the practical way: same method every time, in every store.

Reading the number

One store's share means little; the change across visits and areas matters. A falling share in one area usually points to a rival's push there.
