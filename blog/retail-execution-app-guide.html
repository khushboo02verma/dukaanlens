Retail execution apps: what they do, and where shelf photos fit

DukaanLens team · 5 min read

A retail execution app is what a field salesman uses on a store visit: the beat plan, the order, the scheme, and the record of what he saw. Most teams already use one for orders. Fewer use it to see the shelf.

What a good one covers

  • The beat: which stores to visit today and in what order.
  • The order: what the store buys, with the running schemes.
  • The shelf: what is on it, what is missing, and what rivals did.
  • The record: a visit that can be checked later, with time and place.

Where shelf photos fit

Orders tell you what was sold into the store. The shelf tells you what the shopper can buy. A photo in the same visit connects the two: a store that orders but never shows the product on the shelf is a problem an order report will not show.

What to ask

  1. Does the shelf check add time to the visit, and how much?
  2. Does it work offline in areas with weak network?
  3. Can the salesman act on the result in the same visit?

This guide is part of a test site. If you are choosing shelf audit software, compare real vendors with the questions above.
