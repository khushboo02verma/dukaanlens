A perfect store checklist for FMCG sales teams

DukaanLens team · 4 min read

A perfect store checklist is the short list a salesman checks in every store. It works best when it is short, the same everywhere, and can be checked from what is in front of him.

The checklist

  1. Availability: every must-have product is on the shelf.
  2. Visibility: your products are at eye level and facing the shopper.
  3. Share: your share of the category shelf did not fall since the last visit.
  4. Price: the label matches your price or the running scheme.
  5. Promotion: paid displays and posters are where they should be.

Keeping it honest

A checklist ticked from memory is the weakest record there is. A photo of the shelf, taken in the visit, lets anyone check the ticks later.

This guide is part of a test site. If you are choosing shelf audit software, compare real vendors with the questions above.
