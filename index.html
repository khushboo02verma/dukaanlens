Questions sales heads ask

What if a salesman arranges the shelf only for the photo?

One staged photo is easy; staying unnoticed is not. Photos come only from the in-app camera with time and location, the app asks for surprise re-checks, and a store whose shelf always looks perfect while its sales fall is flagged for review.

Does it work in small kirana stores, not only supermarkets?

It is designed for crowded, mixed shelves and phone photos in normal store light.

Do we need new hardware?

No. It runs on the phones your sales team already carries.

Is this a real product?

No. DukaanLens is a test company used to try out Growth OS. It has no customers, and nothing here is for sale.
