Why it matters

A product missing from the shelf is a sale lost to another brand. In kirana stores it is usually found weeks later, in the sales numbers.

How it works

01

The shelf is photographed

The salesman takes one photo in the in-app camera during his normal visit.

02

Your range is checked

Each product the store should carry is looked for on the shelf.

03

Gaps are flagged before he leaves

Missing products are shown to the salesman, so he can take an order on the spot.

Questions

How does it know what the store should carry?

From the list of products you sell to that store or channel, set up once.

What if the product is in the store's back room?

The report says it is not on the shelf; the salesman can mark it as in stock behind the counter.
