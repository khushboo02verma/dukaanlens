Why it matters

A scheme the shopper never sees does not move sales. Wrong or missing price labels are common and rarely reported.

How it works

01

Labels are read

Price labels on the shelf are read from the photo.

02

Compared with your price list

Each label is compared with your price or the scheme running that week.

03

Mismatches are flagged

Stores with a wrong or missing label are listed for the next visit.

Questions

What if there is no label at all?

A missing label is flagged too; it is often the most common gap.

Does it read handwritten prices?

Clear handwritten labels can be read; unclear ones are flagged for a person to check.
