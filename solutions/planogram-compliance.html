Why it matters

Brands pay for shelf position: eye level, next to a hero product, at the front. Whether that position is kept is rarely checked.

How it works

03

Breaks are listed

Products out of place are listed with the photo, for the salesman to fix.

Questions

Do kirana stores even have planograms?

Usually not a formal one, but paid displays and eye-level deals do. Those are what is checked.

Can different store types have different layouts?

Yes, one layout per store type or per paid display.
