Why it matters

Rivals win shelf space store by store, often with a small incentive to the shopkeeper. Without measurement, it is only noticed when sales fall.

How it works

01

Every facing is counted

Your products and rivals' products are counted on the shelf in the photo.

02

Space is compared

Your share is worked out per category, per store.

03

Changes are shown over time

Stores where your share dropped since the last visit are listed first.

Questions

Does it name the rival brands?

Yes, rival products are recognised as rival brands, so you see who took the space.

Is share of shelf the same as market share?

No. It is what a shopper sees on the shelf, which often moves before market share does.
